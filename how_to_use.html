How to Use the School Portal

Hello, {{ user['first_name'] }}. Follow the steps for your account type.

Start here

  1. Use the menu on the left to open Home, Academics, Profile, or the other pages available to your role.
  2. After submitting a form, look for a message at the top of the page to confirm success or explain an error.
  3. Choose Sign Out when you have finished using the portal.
  4. Use Change Password to replace your password, especially if it was shared or used as an example.
{% if user['role'] == 'admin' %}

Administrator steps

  1. Open Manage Accounts to create staff and student accounts. Complete the required details and set a password for each new account.
  2. Open Academics. Add each subject/class with its section, and optionally assign an active staff member.
  3. In Academics, enroll active students in classes. A student needs an enrollment before attendance or grades can be recorded for that class.
  4. Use the timetable form to choose a class, day, start/end time, and room.
  5. Record attendance and grades in Academics. Pick the class and an enrolled student before saving.
  6. Open Announcements to publish a title and message for the school community.
  7. To disable an account, deactivate it from Manage Accounts. Deleting an account is permanent and may remove related school records.
{% elif user['role'] == 'staff' %}

Staff steps

  1. Open Academics to create a subject/class. Classes you create are assigned to your account.
  2. Enroll active students in your classes. Students must be enrolled before you can record their attendance or grades.
  3. Use the timetable form to add class meeting days, times, and rooms.
  4. Use Record attendance to select your class, enrolled student, date, and status. Use the grade form to record an assessment score.
  5. Open Students and select Edit student to update a student's personal details.
  6. Use Announcements to share school updates.
{% else %}

Student steps

  1. Open Academics to view classes you are enrolled in, their timetable, your attendance, and your grades.
  2. Open Profile and choose Edit Profile to update your personal details.
  3. Read the latest school announcements on your Home dashboard.
  4. If a class or record is missing or incorrect, contact a school staff member.
{% endif %}

Common questions

  • I cannot find a class. Students must be enrolled in a class. Staff can only see classes assigned to them.
  • I cannot record attendance. Confirm the class has at least one active enrolled student, then select that class and student.
  • My form showed an error. Read the message near the top of the page, correct the indicated field, and submit again.
